What we found / 1 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Roseville leads to Bellevue.

A live article discusses cosmetic dentistry in Bellevue, Washington. A local patient may question whether the practice understands their area and choose another dentist. See article ↗

02

The newest listed advice is from 2025.

Visible blog posts stop at February 27, 2025. That misses chances to answer current patient questions. October 20 visible list; not a claim about unlisted or unpublished articles. See blog ↗

03

The team stays in text.

Three clinicians appear in text and photos, without an introduction video. New patients cannot hear their approach before choosing. Reviewed doctors page only; social videos exist. See team page ↗

What we found / 2 of 2

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

Smile photos leave questions.

The gallery lacks treatment names, timing and case stories. Patients comparing cosmetic care cannot see what was involved or what they should ask at a consultation. See gallery ↗

05

10.3 seconds in the test.

Homepage main content appeared after 10.3 seconds in our phone simulation. Visitors may leave before seeing your care. October 20, one slow-4G test; not full-page loading or every visitor’s wait. See mobile test ↗

06

Two booking choices create a pause.

“Request an Appointment” and “Schedule ONLINE” do not explain which to choose. Patients may abandon the choice or mistake a request for a confirmed time. See homepage ↗
